Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert HEIC to PNG for free?

Use HoneyConvert.com - a free online tool. Simply drag and drop your HEIC files, select PNG as the output format, and click Convert. Your PNG files download instantly. No registration, no software installation, no limits.

Does converting HEIC to PNG lose quality?

No, converting HEIC to PNG does not lose quality. PNG is a lossless format, meaning it preserves all image data from the original HEIC file. The visual quality remains identical. However, PNG files are typically larger than HEIC files.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files to PNG at once?

Yes! HoneyConvert supports batch conversion. Select multiple HEIC files at once, and they'll all be converted to PNG. Multiple files are automatically packaged into a ZIP archive for easy download.

Why are my iPhone photos HEIC instead of PNG?

Apple uses HEIC as the default format because it's about 50% smaller than JPEG while maintaining the same quality. This saves storage space on your iPhone. You can change this in Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible to capture in JPEG instead.

Is it safe to convert HEIC files online?

Yes, when using reputable services like HoneyConvert. Your files are processed securely over HTTPS and automatically deleted immediately after conversion. We never store, share, or access your photos.

Should I convert to PNG, JPEG, or WebP?

PNG: Best quality (lossless), larger files. Good for graphics and when quality is paramount.
JPEG: Smaller files, slight quality loss. Great for photos you'll share or upload.
WebP: Modern format, smallest files with good quality. Best for web use.

Why can't Windows open my HEIC files?

Windows doesn't include HEIC support by default. You need to install the "HEIF Image Extensions" from the Microsoft Store. Alternatively, convert your HEIC files to PNG using an online converter for instant compatibility.

How long does HEIC to PNG conversion take?

Using HoneyConvert, most conversions complete in under 10 seconds. The exact time depends on file size and your internet connection. Batch conversions of 10+ files typically take under 30 seconds.
