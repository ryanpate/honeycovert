Why Convert HEIC to PNG?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's default photo format for iPhones and iPads. While it offers excellent compression, many Windows PCs, older software, and websites don't support HEIC files natively.

PNG (Portable Network Graphics) is universally compatible and uses lossless compression, making it perfect for preserving image quality when converting from HEIC.

HEIC vs PNG: Key Differences

  • File Size: HEIC files are typically 50% smaller than PNG
  • Compatibility: PNG works everywhere; HEIC requires iOS 11+ or special software
  • Quality: Both can store high-quality images without visible loss
  • Transparency: PNG supports transparency; HEIC does too but with less software support
  • Editing: PNG is universally supported by all image editors

Does converting HEIC to PNG lose quality?
No. PNG uses lossless compression, which means converting HEIC to PNG preserves the full image quality. Every pixel is maintained exactly as in the original.
Can I convert multiple HEIC files to PNG at once?
Yes! Our converter supports batch conversion. Select multiple HEIC files and convert them all to PNG simultaneously. Multiple files are downloaded as a ZIP archive.
Is it safe to upload my photos?
Absolutely. Your files are processed securely and deleted immediately after conversion. We never store, view, or share your photos.
Why are my iPhone photos in HEIC format?
Apple uses HEIC as the default format because it offers better compression (smaller files) while maintaining quality. However, this can cause compatibility issues with non-Apple devices. Learn more about why iPhone uses HEIC.
